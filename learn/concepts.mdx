## Fund

A fund is one isolated deployment created by `SyndicateFactory`. It has its own vault, withdrawal queue, and governor. Governance parameters and proposal IDs belong to that fund, not to a global governor.

## Vault and shares

`SyndicateVault` is an ERC-4626 vault. It holds the fund asset and issues ERC-20 shares. Those shares represent a claim on vault assets and carry voting power through ERC20Votes checkpoints.

Your current share balance and your voting power can differ. Voting power is read from the checkpoint selected by the governor for a proposal.

## Proposal

A proposal defines:

- calls to execute after a successful vote
- calls or a strategy path used to settle
- a fixed strategy duration
- metadata that explains the rationale and risk

Proposal IDs are scoped to a vault. Always provide the vault address when a command or integration could otherwise be ambiguous.

## Strategy template and clone

A template is allowlisted implementation code. `StrategyFactory` creates an isolated clone and initializes it atomically. A clone cannot move vault assets until governance approves calls that interact with it.

<Info>
  **Portfolio is the only strategy template deployed on the current testnet.** Source code for other templates does not make them available.
</Info>

## Per-fund governor

Each fund receives a governor proxy. The shared `GovernorBeacon` controls governor implementation upgrades, while proposal state and voting configuration remain isolated per fund.

The governor enforces voting, execution, strategy duration, settlement, and exceptional recovery paths.

## Guardians and staked WOOD

Guardians bond WOOD through `StakedWood`. Their non-transferable staking balance determines whether they can participate in reviews, subject to registry rules. Delegators can assign stake to a guardian without transferring governance custody to an agent.

`GuardianRegistry` handles guardian status, review thresholds, delegation accounting, and emergency authority. Guardian rewards are distributed offchain through Merkl rather than minted by the fund vault.

## Liquidity lanes

Sherwood changes its liquidity path while a proposal is active.

| State | Deposit or redemption path | Pricing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No active proposal | Instant ERC-4626 flow | Current vault conversion |
| Active proposal, priceable strategy | Lane A may permit an instant flow | Live strategy value, when the required pricing adapters exist |
| Active proposal, any strategy | Lane B asynchronous queue | Frozen per-proposal settlement price |

On the current testnet, Portfolio uses Lane B. Do not assume Lane A is available just because the contracts implement it.

## Settlement

Execution sends assets into the approved strategy path. Settlement returns assets or proves final strategy value, freezes the proposal's conversion price, and unlocks queued claims. A proposal is not economically complete until settlement succeeds.

## Token vesting

<Warning>
  Token vesting is not deployed on the current Robinhood L2 Testnet fund deployment. The canonical registry has no `VESTING_FACTORY` or `TOKEN_VESTING_IMPL` address. Do not create or fund a grant until those addresses are published in [deployments](/reference/deployments).
</Warning>

Token vesting is separate from the fund and vault system. It uses two contracts:

- `TokenVesting` is a minimal ERC-1167 clone created for one grant. Its owner, beneficiary, token, start time, cliff, duration, and cancellation setting are fixed at initialization. Vesting is linear from the start time. Before the cliff nothing is claimable; at the cliff, the amount accrued since the start unlocks at once.
- `VestingFactory` is permissionless and unowned. It creates and initializes a wallet, records it for the beneficiary, and can fund it in the same transaction. A zero-amount grant creates an unfunded shell that can receive tokens later.

Anyone can call `release()`, but the transfer always goes to the fixed beneficiary. Funding added before cancellation extends the same balance-derived vesting curve rather than creating a new schedule.

For a cancelable grant, only the fixed owner can cancel. Cancellation freezes the vested amount, leaves it claimable by the beneficiary, and returns the unvested balance to the owner. The owner cannot be rotated, so use a durable address such as a multisig.

Use plain ERC-20 tokens without blacklisting, rebasing, transfer fees, or other transfer restrictions. The wallet has no sweep or rescue function. Unrelated tokens and tokens transferred after cancellation can become unrecoverable.
