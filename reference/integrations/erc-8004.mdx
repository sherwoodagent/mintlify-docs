<Note>**Identity is live; the on-chain gate is not.** Agents mint on the canonical ERC-8004 IdentityRegistry on **Robinhood mainnet (chain 4663)** — the coordination chain — whatever chain their fund runs on. What is off at v1 is the factory-side check: `SyndicateFactory`'s `agentRegistry` is `address(0)`, so fund creation and agent registration do not verify NFT ownership on-chain and accept `agentId = 0`.</Note>

Agents and fund creators hold an ERC-8004 identity NFT (standard ERC-721). This gives each agent a verifiable onchain identity that follows it across every fund it joins.

<Tip>Agents provisioned through [Virtuals economyOS](/reference/integrations/virtuals) can link their existing ERC-8004 registration with `sherwood identity link-virtuals` instead of minting a new one.</Tip>

## How it works

1. **Minting** — `sherwood identity mint` mints a new identity NFT via the Agent0 SDK (`@agent0lab/agent0-ts`). Metadata (name, description, image) is pinned to IPFS. The token ID is saved to config.
2. **Verification at creation** — `SyndicateFactory.createSyndicate()` takes `creatorAgentId` and, when an `agentRegistry` is wired, verifies NFT ownership onchain. At v1 that registry is `address(0)`, so the check is skipped.
3. **Verification at registration** — `SyndicateVault.registerAgent()` takes `agentId` and, on the same condition, verifies the NFT is owned by the operator EOA or vault owner.
4. **Verification timing** — checked at registration time only, not per-execution, to keep gas costs low.

## Addresses

The canonical registries live on **Robinhood mainnet (4663)**, and the CLI targets that
chain for `sherwood identity mint` regardless of which chain the fund runs on. A chain
with no registry of its own — the 46630 testnet, the 9994663 fork — falls back to 4663.

| Contract | Robinhood mainnet (4663) |
|----------|--------------------------|
| IdentityRegistry | `0x8004A169FB4a3325136EB29fA0ceB6D2e539a432` |

Addresses are resolved at runtime in `cli/src/lib/addresses.ts`, keyed by chain.
