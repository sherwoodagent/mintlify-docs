title: "Tiers and coverage"
description: "Why uncertified strategies need full guardian coverage, why guardians lock WOOD to approve, and how to size maxCapitalBps, tier2CallCapBps and minBufferBps so your first proposal does not revert."
---

Before a proposal executes, staked guardians must underwrite what its calls could extract, with their own WOOD. The protocol prices that with **tiers**, and the vault owner bounds it with three **caps**. This page explains both, and how to set the caps so a normal proposal goes through.

## Tiers price what a call could extract

`TierRegistry` assigns each `(target, selector)` pair, or a strategy template class, a tier and an extractable bound in bps:

- **Tier 0 and 1** are certified. The Safe proposes a certification and can apply it only after `certifyDelay` (3 days), so a grant cannot be slipped in under a live proposal.
- **Tier 2** is everything uncertified. It is priced at the full notional: 10,000 bps.

**On 4663 today, every core template is tier 2.** `classTierOf(template, execute|settle)` returns `(2, 10000)` for Portfolio, Morpho Supply and Concentrated Liquidity. Nothing is certified.

## Required coverage

```
requiredCoverage = Σ cap_i × boundBps_i / 10,000   over every execute and settle call
```

`sherwood strategy propose` gives the strategy's execute call the whole `--amount` and gives settle calls a cap of 0. With everything at tier 2, **required coverage is about 1× `--amount`**. Read it per proposal with `getRequiredCoverage(proposalId)` on the vault's governor, or in `sherwood proposal show`.

## Why guardians lock WOOD to approve

An Approve vote declares a WOOD lock (`review-vote --support approve --lock-wood <n>`). The lock is clamped to the guardian's stake minus exposure already open elsewhere (k = 1: one unit of stake backs one unit of exposure at a time).

At execute, the governor adds up `min(lock, stake) × servedWoodPrice` over every approver and compares it with the required USD:

| Coverage raised | Result |
|---|---|
| Full | Executes at the voted size |
| Partial | Executes smaller: `effectiveMaxCapital = maxCapital × raised / required`, and every per-call cap scales with it |
| Zero | Reverts `InsufficientApproveCoverage` |

A shortfall scales instead of reverting so that a thin guardian set can still move small, fully covered amounts. Guardians value WOOD at the same 50% haircut as the [proposer bond](/explanations/proposer-bond).

A lock is held until the end of its 7-day epoch plus the 14-day [challenge window](/explanations/challenge-window): about settle + 14 to 21 days. That is the period in which a conviction could slash it. An expired proposal still holds guardian capacity for that time.

<Warning>
A plain `sherwood proposal execute` does not check coverage. Run `sherwood proposal execute --id <id> --vault <vault> --dry-run` first: it fails when coverage is short.
</Warning>

## The owner's three caps

These bound the blast radius regardless of tier. All are set by the vault owner and **frozen while any proposal is open, Drafts included**.

| Cap | Unset value | What it limits | Setter |
|---|---|---|---|
| `maxCapitalBps` | 10,000 (inert) | One proposal's capital, as a share of TVL | `governor set-max-capital` |
| `tier2CallCapBps` | 10,000 (inert) | Any **single** tier-2 call's cap, as a share of TVL | `governor set-tier2-call-cap` |
| `minBufferBps` | 0 | Share of pre-batch float that must stay idle (max 5000) | `vault set-min-buffer` |

### Sizing `tier2CallCapBps`

The governor checks this at propose, per call:

```
cap_i ≤ totalAssets × tier2CallCapBps / 10,000      else Tier2CallCapExceedsCeiling(i)
```

Because the CLI puts the whole `--amount` on one strategy call, and that call is tier 2, the rule for a single-strategy proposal is:

```
--amount ≤ totalAssets × tier2CallCapBps / 10,000
```

**A value of 200 limits every proposal to 2% of TVL.** On a 90 USDG vault that is 1.80 USDG, so a 60 USDG basket reverts. The CLI does not pre-check this; you find out from an on-chain revert after the strategy clone has already been paid for.

While every template is tier 2, set `tier2CallCapBps` **at least as high as `maxCapitalBps`**. If one proposal may deploy up to 80% of TVL, set both to 8000:

```bash
sherwood --calldata-only governor set-max-capital    --vault $VAULT --bps 8000
sherwood --calldata-only governor set-tier2-call-cap --vault $VAULT --bps 8000
```

Lower `tier2CallCapBps` below `maxCapitalBps` only if you want to force multi-call proposals or wait for certified templates. CLI 0.95.0 still prints `200` as a post-create suggestion; do not follow it.

### Sizing `minBufferBps`

At most `(1 − minBufferBps)` of the pre-batch float can leave in a batch. 500 (5%) is a reasonable buffer. **Never raise it while a proposal is open**: settle, `unstick` and emergency finalize all revert until it is lowered again.

## Protocol-wide ceilings

- **$1,000,000 covered TVL per proposal** (`coveredTvlCapUsd`). It bounds what one proposal can ask the guardian set to underwrite. Above it, propose reverts `CoveredTvlCapExceeded`.
- **60-day coverage horizon.** `executeBy + duration` must be within 60 days of now (`CoverageHorizonExceeded`), so guardian locks cannot be pinned indefinitely.
