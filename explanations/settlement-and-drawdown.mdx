Settlement unwinds a strategy back into the vault, takes fees and fixes the price that queued redeems receive. It can also **refuse** to run when the vault has lost more than the proposal said it might. This page explains why.

## The drawdown floor is on the vault's share price

Every proposal declares `maxDrawdownBps` (`--max-drawdown-bps`, default 2000). Settle reverts `SettlePriceBelowFloor` when:

```
pricePerShare < ppsAtExecute × (1 − min(maxDrawdownBps, 9000) / 10,000)
```

It is measured on the **whole vault's** price per share between execute and settle. It is **not** a percentage of `--amount`.

### Why it exists

A settle freezes the price that queued redeems receive. Anyone can settle once the duration ends. Without a floor, a settle run during a flash crash, or after a drain, could stamp a near-zero price, and an attacker could then buy shares cheaply against it. The 9,000 bps hard cap does not close that attack completely; it prices it.

### Declare it honestly

Idle assets in the vault count in the share price, so the same bps tolerates a bigger loss on the strategy itself.

Example: a 90 USDG vault deploys 60 USDG with `--max-drawdown-bps 1500`. The floor is 15% of 90 = 13.5 USDG. On the 60 USDG basket that is a 22.5% loss before settle refuses.

Voters and guardians read the declared drawdown as a promise. Size it from your kill criteria on the whole vault, not on the basket.

## Exits when the floor blocks settle

When the share price is below the floor, a normal settle cannot run. In order of preference:

1. **`unstick`** (owner). Replays the voted settle calls with the drawdown tolerance fixed at the 9,000 bps maximum (`MAX_STAMP_DRAWDOWN_BPS`), so it runs unless the share price fell more than 90%. It needs no new calldata and no bond.
2. **Emergency settle** (owner). `emergencySettleWithCalls` commits new calldata and opens a guardian review; `finalizeEmergencySettle` runs it after the review if guardians did not block. A guardian block burns the whole [owner stake](/explanations/owner-stake).

## Who settles, and when

- **The proposer** can settle from **1 hour** after execute. Before that, settle reverts `StrategyDurationNotElapsed`. The hour stops an execute-then-settle skim in one block.
- **Anyone** can settle after the full strategy duration.
- **Nothing settles automatically.** If nobody calls settle, the strategy keeps running and the management fee keeps accruing.
- A settle batch must have zero net outflow and must leave the strategy with `executed() == false`, else `StrategyNotSettled`.

## Fees

| Fee | Rate | When |
|---|---|---|
| Management | 200 bps/yr on assets | Accrues only from execute to settle. The gap between proposals accrues nothing. |
| Performance | `agentFeeBps` (default 2000, max 2500), clamped to `maxPerformanceFeeBps` (2000) | On the smaller of profit above the high-water mark and this proposal's P&L. A fund that fell and recovered has already paid for that ground. |

Splits are snapshotted at propose:

- Management: agent 60%, protocol 20%, guardians 20%.
- Performance: agent 50%, protocol 15%, guardians 25%, fund owner 10%.

## Queued redeems

Redeems queued during a proposal are stamped at settle, after fees, at the realized price. A redeem queued under a proposal that ends Cancelled, Rejected or Expired is never stamped. Its holder must cancel it to get the shares back.
