---
title: "Oracle staleness"
description: "Why price feeds older than 26 hours stop stock baskets, where the weekend gap comes from, and how to time proposals around it."
---

Sherwood refuses to trade or value against a price older than **26 hours**. Stock feeds stop publishing while US markets are closed, so this one rule produces a regular weekend window when stock baskets cannot move. This page explains the rule and how to plan around it.

## One flat bound: 26 hours

26 hours is the Chainlink 24-hour heartbeat plus 2 hours of grace. Exactly 24 hours would make every read revert whenever a publish ran a little late.

The bound applies in two places:

| Where | Checked on | Revert |
|---|---|---|
| `PortfolioStrategy` (`MAX_PUSH_PRICE_AGE`) | init, execute, settle, rebalance | `StalePrice` |
| `ExposureLedger` asset feed | every propose and every coverage read | `StalePrice` |

## Why a bound at all

Portfolio swaps are floored at the feed price minus `maxSlippageBps`. That floor only protects the vault if the feed price is close to the market. With a stale price, a swap could clear far from the real market and still pass the check.

## Where the weekend gap comes from

**No contract or CLI code knows about weekends.** The gap is a consequence of the 26-hour rule:

1. Stock feeds publish for the last time on Friday after the US close.
2. About 26 hours later (around Saturday 18:40 UTC; it moves with that last publish), each feed crosses the bound.
3. Every Portfolio that holds a stock then reverts `StalePrice` on execute, settle and rebalance.
4. The first Monday publish clears it. The total gap is about 52 to 55 hours. Market holidays extend it.

`sherwood sources get calendar` serves the live window. You can also compute it yourself from each feed's `updatedAt` + 26 hours.

## The vault asset is gated too

The ledger prices the vault asset (USDG) on every propose, even for a proposal that needs zero coverage. A stale USDG feed blocks **every** propose on every vault until it publishes again.

## What it costs you

- **An execution window that closes inside the gap expires the proposal.** Guardian locks stay booked for their full hold anyway.
- **A settle that falls inside the gap waits.** The management fee keeps accruing until settle.
- **Rebalancing cannot fix it.** Portfolio weights and routes are frozen at init; `rebalanceDelta` can only restore the frozen weights, and the only tunable parameter is the slippage tolerance, which can only be tightened. Depositors approved a basket, not a mandate.

## What to do

- Work back from the window. With a 24-hour vote, 30-minute review and 24-hour execution window, the execution window runs from 24.5 to 48.5 hours after propose. Propose by about Thursday 18:00 UTC, or after the Monday publish.
- Set `--duration` so the planned settle lands on a weekday.
- Check `sherwood sources get prices` before proposing: it shows which feeds are fresh.
- Do not hold a stock across an announced split.
