Guardians who **Approve** are underwriters. `ExposureLedger` is the coverage book: it prices what a proposal can extract, books the WOOD a guardian locks against that price when it approves, and at execute tells the governor how much coverage actually stands behind the calldata.

A coverage **shortfall does not disqualify the proposal**. It **scales** how much capital may leave the vault. Guardian daemons that treat a shortfall as a hard fail are implementing a rule the contracts do not enforce.

The [proposer bond](/protocol/governance/proposer-bond) is a separate WOOD pull at propose, quoted from this same `requiredCoverage`.

<Note>
`ExposureLedger` is part of the launch's second deployment run. Until a governor has a ledger wired, it skips the covered-TVL cap, the horizon, the bond and the execute-time quorum, and runs every proposal at its declared `maxCapital`. Addresses: [Deployments](/reference/deployments).
</Note>

## requiredCoverage

`SyndicateGovernor._snapshotTierAndGate` writes `proposal.requiredCoverage` at propose. Read it later with `getRequiredCoverage(proposalId)`.

Coverage is the **sum of per-call contributions across both execute and settlement calls**:

```solidity
coverage += (cap_i * boundBps_i) / 10_000;
```

`boundBps_i` comes from `TierRegistry.tierOf(target, selector)`:

- **Tier 0 / 1**: the certified extractable bound for that `(target, selector)` pair.
- **Tier 2 or uncertified**: `10_000`, the full notional of the declared cap.

The proposal's tier is the **max** tier across execute **and** settlement calls. An uncertified strategy therefore books roughly twice its deployed capital: the execute leg and the settle leg each price at full notional. The Portfolio class is uncertified at launch; its certification at 2,000 bps is pending. See [Tier policy](/protocol/governance/tier-policy).

A tier-2 call carries a second, independent propose-time limit: its declared `cap_i` must fit inside `totalAssets() × tier2CallCapBps / 10_000`, or propose reverts `Tier2CallCapExceedsCeiling(i)`.

<Note>
Zero coverage is specified, not a hole. An all-zero-cap batch prices to zero regardless of tier. `requiredCoverage == 0` skips the approve-quorum gate and needs no bond; a proposal that can extract nothing has nothing to underwrite. The per-call meter is the protection: `cap_i == 0` makes `BatchExecutorLib` revert `CallCapExceeded` on any outflow.
</Note>

### Propose-time gates (these *do* block)

These run in `_snapshotTierAndGate` against the **declared** coverage, before anyone underwrites:

| Check | Function | Launch value | Revert |
|---|---|---|---|
| Covered-TVL ceiling per proposal | `ExposureLedger.requireWithinCoveredTvlCap(asset, coverage)` | `coveredTvlCapUsd` = $1,000,000 | `CoveredTvlCapExceeded` |
| Booking horizon | `ExposureLedger.requireWithinCoverageHorizon(executeBy, strategyDuration)` | `MAX_COVERAGE_HORIZON` 60 d from now | `CoverageHorizonExceeded` |
| Proposer bond | `ExposureLedger.proposerBondWood(asset, coverage)` → `ProposerBondEscrow.lockBond` | `proposerBondBps` 100 | `NoWoodPrice` if WOOD is unpriceable; the token's `transferFrom` revert if the wallet is short |

`coveredTvlCapUsd == 0` is fail-closed: nothing with non-zero coverage is proposable until the owner seeds the cap.

At execute, `_resolveTierAndCoverage` runs again. If the live tier is higher than the snapshot, `executeProposal` reverts `TierRegressed`; if live coverage is higher, `CoverageRegressed`. Those are **regression** guards (a demotion between propose and execute), not the shortfall path.

## Approve is underwriting

The hook is `GuardianRegistry.voteOnProposal(governor, proposalId, support, lockWood)` → `ExposureLedger.recordApproval(governor, proposalId, guardian, lockWood)`.

On a first `Approve`, and on a `Block` → `Approve` change, the registry writes its own vote state **then** calls the ledger. The ledger call is not wrapped: if it reverts, the vote reverts, so an approver slot is never seated without a lock behind it.

**The guardian declares the size of its own bet.** `lockWood` is the fourth argument of the vote, the WOOD the approver puts behind *this* proposal. It is ignored on a Block; pass `0`. The ledger books:

```solidity
cap  = kNumerator * swood.guardianStake(guardian);   // kNumerator = 1
free = cap - openExposure(guardian);                 // floored at 0
lock = min(lockWood, free);
```

With `kNumerator = 1`, a guardian's locks never sum to more than its stake, so one conviction cannot leave its other proposals under-covered.

**Over-declaring clamps; under-declaring is refused.** A lock larger than the free budget books less, and the difference surfaces at execute as a shortfall. A lock that is too small reverts `ApproveLockBelowFloor`:

- `shareUsd = ceil(needUsd / 100)`, one approver's share if a hundred carried the book (`MAX_APPROVERS_PER_PROPOSAL` is 100; blockers are uncapped).
- `budgetUsd` is the guardian's whole cap, `min(cap, stake) × woodPriceX8 / 1e8`.
- `floorUsd = shareUsd` if `shareUsd < budgetUsd`, or if 50 or more approvers are already booked; otherwise `floorUsd = budgetUsd`. A guardian whose whole budget is below one share is admitted with its whole budget only while fewer than half the slots are taken.
- Reverts if `lock == 0`, `budgetUsd == 0`, or the booked lock is worth less than `floorUsd`.

Every WOOD amount is valued at the haircut `woodPriceX8()`, so a starved or inflated feed can refuse a slot but never enlarge one.

An Approve also needs the ledger's inputs: it reverts `CoverageInputsUnreadable`, `StalePrice` / `FeedNotConfigured` (the vault-asset feed), `NoWoodPrice` (the WOOD feed) or `CoverageHorizonExceeded`. A Block reads no price, so it keeps working through a pricing outage.

### Lock lifetime

A lock sits in the epoch bucket that holds `executeBy + strategyDuration` and is held until `bucketEnd + challengeWindow`, whether or not the proposal executed. At the launch constants (`epochLength` 7 days, `challengeWindow` 14 days) a lock lives about 23–53 days for a 7- to 30-day strategy, and it keeps counting against the guardian's free budget and blocks `claimUnstakeGuardian` (`CoverageStillOpen`) until then.

- `releaseApproval` is the unwind on `Approve` → `Block`, registry-only; `CoverageFrozen` while a challenge is live.
- `retireApproval` is permissionless once the bucket has aged out; before that it reverts `ChallengeWindowOpen`, and `CoveragePinnedActive` while an inconclusive challenge round pins the lock.
- `openExposure(guardian)` walks the buckets in WOOD, with no price, across a 60-day horizon.

The execute-time quorum reads the ledger's **own** approver list, never the registry, so the two cannot disagree about who covered a proposal.

## Shortfall scales rather than blocks

This is the execute-time rule. Guardian daemons that treat a coverage shortfall as disqualifying are **wrong**.

`IExposureLedger.requireApproveQuorum` is a **coverage measurement with a zero floor**, not an all-or-nothing gate. It returns `(coverageRaisedUsd, requiredCoverageUsd)` so the caller can size execution to a coverage-proportional effective capital. It **reverts only** when the approver set is empty or the raised aggregate is exactly zero (`InsufficientApproveCoverage`). A nonzero-but-partial aggregate is **reported to the caller**.

`SyndicateGovernor._deriveAndStoreEffectiveCapital` is that caller. It runs inside `executeProposal` after `executedAt` is stamped, in the same transaction. The gate runs when:

```solidity
bool gated = ledger != address(0) && proposal.requiredCoverage != 0;
```

There is no tier threshold: every tier is gated. When the gate does not run, `effectiveMaxCapital` stays `maxCapital`. When it does:

```solidity
(coverageRaisedUsd, requiredCoverageUsd) = IExposureLedger(ledger)
    .requireApproveQuorum(address(this), proposalId, asset, proposal.requiredCoverage);

bool scale = coverageRaisedUsd < requiredCoverageUsd;
uint256 effectiveMaxCapital = scale
    ? (maxCapital * coverageRaisedUsd) / requiredCoverageUsd
    : maxCapital;
```

That is floor integer division. Dust coverage can floor `effectiveMaxCapital` to zero, a zero net-outflow cap, fail-closed. The division never sees a zero denominator on the scaling branch: `requireApproveQuorum` already reverted on a raised aggregate of exactly zero.

The same ratio scales every per-call cap via `_scaleCaps` (`(caps[i] * raised) / required`). Scaled **settlement** caps are persisted so `settleProposal` reuses them verbatim and **never recomputes** coverage. A coverage drop between execute and settle cannot cap the unwind below the size legitimately deployed.

`executeProposal` emits `EffectiveMaxCapitalSet(proposalId, declaredMaxCapital, effectiveMaxCapital, coverageRaisedUsd, requiredCoverageUsd)`.

<Warning>
Silence still does not pass a gated proposal. Empty approvers or a raised aggregate of exactly zero **does** revert `InsufficientApproveCoverage`. The proposal stays `Approved` and expires at `executeBy` unless a covering Approve arrives. That is "no underwriter on the hook," not a shortfall. A **partial** book is the shortfall case, and it scales.
</Warning>

### What `requireApproveQuorum` measures

`requiredCoverageUsd = coverageUsd(asset, requiredCoverage)`, the vault asset's Chainlink feed to USD (18 decimals), stale past 26 hours.

Each approver contributes `min(lock, live slashable stake) × woodPriceX8() / 1e8`: the lock it declared, valued at the haircut price now, and no more than its stake is worth now. A guardian whose lock is worth less than when it declared it (an unstake request, a WOOD price fall) counts at the shrunken live value, so coverage must hold in dollars at execution. The loop sums locks; there is no pro-rating and no cross-proposal sharing.

This is **not an indemnity**. `coverageRaisedUsd` is bonded conviction standing behind the proposal. Slash proceeds are **burned**, not paid to anyone harmed. `requiredCoverage` is the price of admission to a tier, expressed in the same dollars a loss would be because that is the natural scale, not because the two net out.

## WOOD pricing

`woodPriceX8()` is `min(feed, woodUsdPriceX8 cap) × woodHaircutBps / 10_000`, floored at 1, and reverts `NoWoodPrice` when the cap is zero or the feed cannot answer.

| Input | Launch value |
|-------|--------------|
| Feed | `WoodV3TwapFeed` (since 2026-10-13): the WOOD/WETH Uniswap V3 pool's 1-hour TWAP, converted through Chainlink ETH/USD; keeperless, read live from the pool's observation ring. It replaced the run-1 `WoodPoolFeed` (24-hour two-pool snapshots, permissionless `update()`) |
| `woodHaircutBps` | 5,000 (50%); bounds 5,000 – 10,000 |
| Cap (`setWoodUsdPrice`) | 2× the feed's TWAP when the feed was swapped (2026-10-13), owner-set thereafter |
| Feed max delay | 1 h (the feed stamps every read with the current block time) |

`NoWoodPrice` halts every Approve, every propose and execute with non-zero coverage, challenge filing and fee weights, while Block votes, settlement and the queue keep working.
