The proposer bond is a WOOD deposit the governor pulls into `ProposerBondEscrow` inside `propose`. It is released when the proposal reaches a terminal state and forfeited only on a challenge conviction. It is the second WOOD pull a vault creator makes; the first is the owner stake. [Why every proposal locks WOOD](/explanations/proposer-bond) explains why the bond exists and why it is quoted at half price.

## Who needs WOOD

| Pull | When | Where | Size at launch | Returned? |
|------|------|-------|----------------|-----------|
| Creation fee | `createSyndicate` | `SyndicateFactory` → fee recipient | 1,000,000 WOOD, waived once for a sponsored creator (`creationSponsored`) | No |
| Owner stake | before `createSyndicate` | `StakedWood.prepareOwnerStake`, bound by the factory at creation | `minOwnerStake` = 10,000 WOOD flat; `MIN_OWNER_BOND_FLOOR` of 1,000 WOOD is unconditional; no TVL scaling | Yes, after the 7-day cooldown (`requestUnstakeOwner` → `claimUnstakeOwner`); refused while the vault has an open proposal |
| **Proposer bond** | `propose` | `ProposerBondEscrow.lockBond` | Quoted, see below | Yes, on any terminal state |

Depositors need no WOOD. Guardians stake separately (`minGuardianStake` 10,000 WOOD), see [Guardian Review](/protocol/governance/guardian-review).

The owner stake is slashed only when guardians block an owner emergency settle. The burn takes effect when the blocked review resolves through the permissionless `GuardianRegistry.resolveEmergencyReview`, or when the owner opens the next emergency settle; a `finalizeEmergencySettle` on a blocked review reverts `EmergencySettleBlocked`, and that revert rolls back its own burn. See [Owner stake](/explanations/owner-stake). Proposing and executing require the stake to be live (`OwnerBondNotLive`): an owner who has requested an unstake cannot propose until `cancelUnstakeOwner`.

## Quote

```text
proposerBondWood(asset, requiredCoverage)
  = floor(coverageUsd(asset, requiredCoverage) × proposerBondBps / 10_000) × 1e8 / woodPriceX8()
```

- `proposerBondBps` is **100 (1%)**, an `ExposureLedger` parameter (setter bound ≤ 10,000).
- [`requiredCoverage`](/protocol/governance/coverage) is the sum over execute **and** settlement calls of `cap_i × boundBps_i / 10_000`. An uncertified (tier 2) call prices at full notional. `sherwood strategy propose` gives the strategy's execute call the whole `--amount` and every settle call a cap of 0, so a CLI-built proposal on any core template books about its deployed capital (1×).
- `woodPriceX8()` is the **haircut** price: `min(feed, cap) × woodHaircutBps / 10_000`, with `woodHaircutBps` 5,000 (50%, also the contract floor) and the cap seated at 2× spot when the feed was set (`CAP_OVER_SPOT_BPS` 20,000). The haircut halves the dollar value of each WOOD, so the WOOD count is twice what the market price implies. The reason is in the ledger source: collateral wants a floor, not a quote. The cap stops a pumped WOOD price from shrinking bonds.
- A zero-coverage proposal quotes zero and needs no price. Any other proposal reverts `NoWoodPrice` while WOOD is unpriceable (feed older than its max delay, pool depth below the floors, or cap unset).

Put together: bond ≈ 1% × required coverage ÷ (0.5 × WOOD price). Nothing is certified on 4663 today, so for a CLI-built proposal on any core template the bond is **about 2% of `--amount`, in WOOD at market price**. Example (2026-10-15): a 60 USDG basket quoted 371 WOOD, about $1.20 at the feed price that day.

The number scales with the book and with the WOOD price. A WOOD price fall raises the WOOD count of every new bond. **Never treat a fixed WOOD figure as the requirement**: quote it live from `ExposureLedger.proposerBondWood` (`sherwood strategy propose` prints it) at the moment you propose.

<Tip>
**Reserve rule for creators.** Hold, in the creator wallet and outside the vault: 10,000 WOOD for the owner stake plus about 3–4% of the capital you plan to deploy, in WOOD. That covers one bond with room for a 50% WOOD fall. A settled bond stays locked for the 14-day challenge window, so back-to-back proposals keep about `(duration + 14d) / cadence` bonds locked at once; size for that. Add 1,000,000 WOOD if your creation is not sponsored. Acquisition is a Uniswap swap on Robinhood Chain; the token and pool addresses are on [Deployments](/reference/deployments).
</Tip>

## Pull and release

- **Pull.** `lockBond(proposalId, proposer, amount)` runs inside `propose` as a `safeTransferFrom` from the proposer. The wallet must **hold** the WOOD and have approved the escrow; an allowance alone reverts in the token. The CLI quotes the bond, sets the allowance and refuses early if the balance is short (`InsufficientProposerBondWood` is a CLI-side name, not a contract error).
- **Release.** `reclaimProposerBond(proposalId)` is permissionless and always pays the recorded proposer. The state must be `Rejected`, `Expired`, `Cancelled` or `Settled` (`ProposalNotTerminal`); a zero bond reverts `NoBondToReclaim`. The first three release immediately. A **Settled** proposal passes three gates, each reverting `ChallengeWindowOpen` while it fails:
  1. `now ≥ executedAt + strategyDuration + ExposureLedger.challengeWindow()` (14 days);
  2. the proposal's coverage is not frozen by a live challenge;
  3. `now` is past both the game's `executedAt + strategyDuration + ChallengeGame.challengeWindow()` and any re-armed `challengeableUntil`.
- **Forfeit.** Only a challenge conviction (a guilty verdict, or silence for `autoSlashDelay` after a filing) forfeits the bond: `forfeitBond` pays `prosecutorFeeBps` (20%) to the challenger and burns the rest. A later `reclaimProposerBond` then emits `ProposerBondForfeitureAcknowledged` and clears the record. Challenge filings are paused until the juror reserve is 30 days old, so no bond can be forfeited until they are unpaused. See [Challenge window](/explanations/challenge-window).

A guardian coverage shortfall at execute [scales the proposal](/protocol/governance/coverage#shortfall-scales-rather-than-blocks); it does not resize the bond.

`ExposureLedger` and `ProposerBondEscrow` are deployed on 4663; their addresses are in `chains/4663.json` and on [Deployments](/reference/deployments). The proposer bond is not a governor setter and does not appear in `sherwood governor info`.
