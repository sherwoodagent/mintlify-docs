> Capital secures capital — \$WOOD is the stake behind every guardian verdict.

Guardian review sits between a proposal's voting window and its execution window. Guardians are staked, slashable third parties who vote `Approve` or `Block` on the exact calldata depositors just approved. An `Approve` is underwriting: it locks WOOD behind the proposal and that lock is what a block or a conviction burns. Vault owners also post a slashable WOOD stake before their vault can create proposals; it is burned if they abuse the emergency-settle escape hatch.

## Where it sits in the lifecycle

```text
Pending → GuardianReview → Approved → Executed → Settled
              │                                     └ challenge window (14 d)
              ├ Rejected (block quorum → approvers slashed)
              └ Approved (no block quorum)
```

- Guardians cannot act before voting ends; they review calldata that has already passed the depositor vote.
- The owner's `vetoProposal` is limited to `Pending`. Once in `GuardianReview`, only the block quorum can reject.
- After execution, `unstick` re-runs the pre-committed unwind calls with no review; custom settlement calldata goes through a separate, block-only review window (see [Settlement](/protocol/governance/settlement#settlement-paths)).
- After settlement, a 14-day challenge window lets anyone bond a challenge against the approvers. Filings are paused through the private beta.

## Becoming a guardian

`StakedWood.stakeAsGuardian(amount, agentId)` is permissionless: no cap, no allowlist, only a stake floor. A guardian is active while `stakedAmount > 0` and no unstake is pending.

| Parameter | Launch value | Bounds |
|---|---|---|
| `minGuardianStake` | 10,000 WOOD | ≥ 1 WOOD; gates entry only (`InsufficientStake`) |
| Unstake cooldown (`setCooldownPeriod`) | 7 days | 1 – 30 days, and ≥ `reviewPeriod` |
| `minSlashBps` / `maxSlashBps` | 1,000 (10%) / 10,000 (100%) | `min ≤ max ≤ 10,000` |

`requestUnstakeGuardian` zeroes the guardian's vote weight at once and starts the cooldown; `claimUnstakeGuardian` pays out after it, and reverts `CoverageStillOpen` while the guardian still has an open or frozen coverage lock. A stake top-up re-anchors the stake age to the stake-weighted average.

## Guardian economics

- **A slice of both protocol fees.** Guardians receive **20% of every management fee** and **25% of every performance fee**, paid onchain in the vault asset to `guardiansFeeRecipient` and distributed off-chain, weekly, via [Merkl](https://merkl.xyz) as WOOD. The governor's `GuardianFeeAccrued` event marks each delivery, and the payout is attributed per approver from the registry's approver views (`getApproverWeights`, `getApproverCoverage`). The management leg matters most: it is charged at every settlement, including flat and losing ones, so the review layer stays funded when the performance leg pays nothing. See [Economics](/protocol/governance/economics).
- **No onchain reward pool and no staking emissions.** Slashed WOOD is burned, never redistributed.
- **You only lose stake for approving.** Blockers are never slashed; approvers lose their lock when the cohort blocks or a challenge convicts.

## How a review runs

| Step | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Register** | Governor, at the transition into `Pending` | `registerReview` records `snapshotAt = block.timestamp − 1` and the window `[voteEnd, reviewEnd)`. |
| **Open** | Permissionless, from `voteEnd` | `openReview(governor, proposalId)` stores the quorum denominator, `getPastTotalVotes(snapshotAt)`, and snapshots `blockQuorumBps` and the slash envelope. Idempotent. |
| **Vote** | Active guardian, `voteEnd ≤ now < reviewEnd` | `voteOnProposal(governor, proposalId, support, lockWood)`: `Approve` with the WOOD to lock, or `Block` (`lockWood` ignored). Weight is the guardian's raw stake at `snapshotAt` (`getPastStake`, no age discount). Vote changes are allowed until the last 10% of the window (`LATE_VOTE_LOCKOUT_BPS`, `VoteChangeLockedOut`). At most 100 approvers per proposal (`NewSideFull`); blockers are uncapped. |
| **Resolve** | Permissionless, from `reviewEnd` | `resolveReview(governor, proposalId)` computes the block quorum, slashes approvers if blocked, and lets the governor commit `Approved` or `Rejected`. `ReviewNotReadyForResolve` before `reviewEnd`. |

<Warning>
**A review nobody opens is a proposal that executes unreviewed.** `voteOnProposal` reverts `ReviewNotOpen` until `openReview` lands, and `resolveReview` returns *cleared* for a review that was never opened. Blocking is **two transactions**: `openReview` from `voteEnd`, then the vote. Both are permissionless, so a guardian that only watches for *opened* reviews cannot be the one that opens them.
</Warning>

**Block quorum.** A review resolves *blocked* when `blockStakeWeight × 10_000 ≥ blockQuorumBps × denominator`, with `blockQuorumBps` 3,000 (30%) at launch and both sides measured at `snapshotAt`. Stake checkpointed before the propose block counts in the denominator whether or not it votes; stake added in that block or later neither votes nor moves the bar. There is no cohort-size floor: only a zero denominator fails open, because nobody could have reviewed.

**Approve needs a price; Block does not.** An `Approve` calls `ExposureLedger.recordApproval` unwrapped, so it reverts with the ledger's `ApproveLockBelowFloor`, `NoWoodPrice`, `StalePrice` or `CoverageHorizonExceeded`. A `Block` reads no price and keeps working through a feed outage. See [Coverage](/protocol/governance/coverage) for the lock rules.

<Note>
Don't poll a proposal's state to detect `Rejected`. After voting ends the state reads `GuardianReview` until resolution; subscribe to `GuardianReviewResolved`, or call `resolveReview` yourself. Timing is fixed at propose: `reviewEnd = voteEnd + reviewPeriod`, `executeBy = reviewEnd + executionWindow`. A registry pause defers open windows; the governor keeps the proposal in `GuardianReview` until the review resolves.
</Note>

**Guardian vote enum** is separate from the governor's `VoteType`: guardians vote `{ None, Approve, Block }`, and a review resolves to `{ Unresolved, Cleared, Blocked }`.

## Vault-owner stake

A creator prepares `minOwnerStake` (10,000 WOOD at launch; `MIN_OWNER_BOND_FLOOR` of 1,000 WOOD is unconditional; no TVL scaling) with `StakedWood.prepareOwnerStake` before `createSyndicate`, and the factory binds it to the new vault. It must be live for the vault to propose or execute (`OwnerBondNotLive`). `requestUnstakeOwner` is refused while the vault has an open proposal (`VaultHasActiveProposal`) and pays out after the 7-day cooldown. The stake exists to make the emergency-settle path costly to abuse; it is the one place an owner can supply arbitrary calldata against fund capital, so it is bonded and guardian-gated.

## Slashing

Slashing is onchain and final: slashed WOOD is **burned** to `0x…dEaD`, not sent to a treasury.

- **Approvers slashed** when a review resolves *blocked*. For each approver the registry asks `StakedWood` for `ceil(lockBps × severity / 10_000)`, where `lockBps` is the approver's lock over its stake and `severity` is a deterministic ramp of how decisively the cohort blocked, clamped into the `[minSlashBps, maxSlashBps]` envelope snapshotted at open. Severity is `minSlashBps` when the block side only just reached the quorum, `maxSlashBps` once it reaches a two-thirds supermajority (`6_667` bps), and `lo + (hi − lo) × t²` in between. Guardians do not choose the penalty, and a tiny lock still costs at least `minSlashBps` of the guardian's stake.
- **Challenge conviction** (`slashVerdict`) burns each approver's lock, clamped into the same envelope, anchored at the stake the guardian held at `executedAt`.
- **Owner stake slashed** when an emergency-settle review resolves blocked: burned in full.
- Approver slashing is bounded by the 100-slot cap to keep gas deterministic; blockers are uncapped, since capping honest defence would be a griefing vector.

## Emergency-settle review

When a strategy's pre-committed unwind calls are broken, the owner can submit custom settlement calldata, but only through a bonded, block-only review.

| Step | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | Governor, from `emergencySettleWithCalls` | Reverts `OwnerBondInsufficient` unless the owner's stake is live and at least `requiredOwnerBond`. Stores the hash of the calls (`EmergencyHashMismatch` on finalize if they differ; at most 64 calls) and opens a `reviewPeriod`-long window. Calls do not execute yet. |
| **Block** | Active guardian | `voteBlockEmergencySettle`: block-only, weighted at the snapshot. No approve side; absence of a block is an implicit allow. |
| **Finalize** | Governor, from `finalizeEmergencySettle` | After the window: if the block quorum was reached the owner's stake is burned and finalize reverts `EmergencySettleBlocked`; otherwise the stored calls run and the proposal settles. |

The owner can self-recall with `cancelEmergencySettle` before the window closes, with no slash. See [Settlement — Settlement paths](/protocol/governance/settlement#settlement-paths).

## After execution: the challenge window

Every executed proposal stays challengeable for `challengeWindow` (14 days) after `executedAt + strategyDuration`. `ChallengeGame.file` is permissionless and bonds 1.5% of the cohort's liability in WOOD; a filing freezes the approvers' locks and starts a 7-day `autoSlashDelay`. Silence convicts: the approvers' locks are burned, the proposer bond is forfeited (20% to the challenger, the rest burned) and the adapter pair is demoted. A counter-bond that fills the pool sends the case to `TokenCourt`, a single WOOD-vote with a 5-day window where the accused, the challenger, the proposer and the counter-bond contributors cannot vote and stake younger than 30 days weighs nothing.

<Info>
**Filings are paused through the private beta** (`ChallengeGame.filingsPaused`). Every court path is unreachable until the protocol multisig unpauses, so approvers carry review-path slash risk only, and proposer bonds on settled proposals are released once the 14-day window has run. The owner stake, the guardian locks and the review quorum are unaffected.
</Info>

## Appeals

Slashing is final at the protocol layer; appeals are reserve-funded refunds, not onchain reversals. The registry owner can call `refundSlash` from a dedicated slash-appeal reserve, capped at **20% of the reserve per epoch** (`MAX_REFUND_PER_EPOCH_BPS`, `RefundCapExceeded`) so a compromised multisig cannot drain it in one call. Anyone can top the reserve up.

## Parameters (launch values)

These are initialization parameters set by the deployment, not contract defaults. All are owner-instant (no onchain timelock; the owner multisig enforces its own delay); the `GuardianRegistry` and `StakedWood` setters emit `ParameterChangeFinalized(paramKey, old, new)`.

| Parameter | Where | Launch value | Bounds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `minGuardianStake` | `StakedWood` | 10,000 WOOD | ≥ 1 WOOD |
| `minOwnerStake` | `StakedWood` | 10,000 WOOD | 0 (open onboarding) or ≥ 1,000 WOOD |
| Unstake cooldown | `StakedWood` | 7 days | 1 – 30 days, ≥ `reviewPeriod` |
| `reviewPeriod` | `GuardianRegistry` | 24 hours | 6 hours – 3 days |
| `blockQuorumBps` | `GuardianRegistry` | 3,000 (30%) | 1,000 – 10,000 |
| `minSlashBps` / `maxSlashBps` | `StakedWood` | 10% / 100% | `min ≤ max ≤ 100%` |
| `MAX_APPROVERS_PER_PROPOSAL` | `GuardianRegistry` | 100 | constant |
| `LATE_VOTE_LOCKOUT_BPS` | `GuardianRegistry` | last 10% of the window | constant |
| `epochLength` | `ExposureLedger` | 7 days | immutable |
| `challengeWindow` | `ExposureLedger` / `ChallengeGame` | 14 days | ≥ `reviewPeriod` + 7 days |
| `coveredTvlCapUsd` | `ExposureLedger` | $1,000,000 per proposal | 0 fails closed |
| `woodHaircutBps` | `ExposureLedger` | 5,000 (50%) | 5,000 – 10,000 |
| `filingsPaused` | `ChallengeGame` | true | — |

Load-bearing constants: the two-thirds supermajority (`6_667` bps) for the severity ceiling, the 20% per-epoch cap on appeal refunds, and a 7-day deadman (`DEADMAN_UNPAUSE_DELAY`) that lets anyone unpause the registry if the owner goes silent. The pause freezes voting and review resolution; it never freezes stake, unstake or claim, so positions are always exitable.

## Thin cohort

There is no minimum cohort stake. Any positive at-open stake decides its own reviews, however small. Only a zero denominator fails open: if nobody held stake at the snapshot, the review is not blocked. The cohort at launch is operator-run, so the review is a security service over proposals submitted by vault owners, not a check on the operator.

## Known limitations

- **Blockers have no stake at risk.** Slashing only hits approvers, so a large-stake party could block proposals at the cost of capital alone. `stakeAsGuardian` is permissionless, so a third party can also raise the denominator the cohort must clear.
- **No ex-post challenge backstop during the beta.** With filings paused, a bad execution that cleared review can only be answered by the vault owner's whitelist and veto, which is why both launch flags are on.
- **Resolve is permissionless but gas-heavy.** If no keeper resolves a blocked review, the next state read forces resolution and that caller pays.
